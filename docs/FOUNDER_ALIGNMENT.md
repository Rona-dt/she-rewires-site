# She Rewires Founder Alignment

Last updated: 2026-08-04

## Status and authority

This document records the founder's approved product and brand direction. It is the highest-priority input for design, content, and implementation decisions. If an implementation idea conflicts with this document, pause and resolve the conflict before changing code.

This is a product source of truth, not a visual mood board. The website must express the decisions below while preserving a maintainable route and content architecture.

## 1. Website purpose

She Rewires is not an event organiser, a traditional Women in Tech community, an NGO showcase, a consultancy, a SaaS product, a membership platform, or an accelerator.

She Rewires is a continuously operating co-building system: a global women-led platform where people, ideas, and ecosystems connect, co-build, and create meaningful impact.

Recommended positioning:

> She Rewires is a global women-led co-building platform connecting people, ideas and ecosystems to cultivate human agency for the AI era.

Human Agency is the future positioning. It is not the first-entry label visitors must understand before they know who She Rewires is.

The website must follow **Known → New**, not Unknown → More Unknown.

## 2. Brand evolution

```text
Women in STEM
    → Women-led global movement
    → Co-building platform
    → Human Agency for the AI era
```

Women is the foundation and must not be weakened by the future-facing language. Human Agency should be introduced as the meaningful evolution of the work, not as a replacement for its origin.

## 3. Audience priority

1. Women in technology and innovation
2. Co-builders and community builders
3. Founders and professionals
4. Corporations and ecosystem partners
5. Universities and institutions
6. Mentors, speakers, and researchers
7. Investors and foundations
8. Anyone interested in human agency

## 4. Desired brand feeling

The experience should feel:

- Human
- Visionary
- Global
- Intelligent
- Purpose-led
- Community-driven
- Warm
- Inclusive

It should avoid feeling:

- Overly corporate
- NGO-like
- Overly feminine or pink
- Like a SaaS landing page
- Like an event registration website
- Dense or crowded
- Abstract without a concrete human meaning

## 5. Homepage narrative

The approved sequence is:

```text
Identity
  → Why
  → Evolution
  → Co-building Model
  → People
  → Projects
  → Partners
  → Stories
  → Join Us
```

The homepage must first answer what She Rewires is and why it exists. Projects, events, stories, partners, and metrics are proof of the system, not its opening identity.

The first viewport should communicate:

> Building the future isn't about waiting for change. It's about co-building it together.

Supporting positioning:

> She Rewires is a global women-led co-building platform connecting people, ideas, and ecosystems to cultivate human agency for the AI era.

Chinese hero direction:

> 未来，不是等待发生，而是共同创造。

### Language behaviour

The bilingual logo lockup is part of the brand identity and may remain visible in both scripts. Page copy is different: each view must use one content language consistently. English is the default locale, and the top-right language control must switch the complete page to Chinese, including navigation, headings, body copy, buttons, forms, metadata, and footer. Do not use `She Rewires / 她原力` as a mixed-language Hero headline. A partial translation or mixed-language state is not acceptable.

## 6. Reference principles

### BEYOND Expo: learn selectively

Learn from its international brand confidence, large-scale hero, editorial composition, whitespace, typography, simple navigation, curated partner presentation, and purposeful motion.

Do not copy its conference-first structure, dense information presentation, event schedule emphasis, or brand identity.

### Women in Tech: learn selectively

Learn from real people, authentic community stories, activity photography, and a global chapter feeling.

Do not copy its visual style, colour system, page hierarchy, event-heavy navigation, or traditional Women in Tech community expression.

### IDEO, TED, Stripe Press, Apple, Notion, Airbnb

Use these references for brand narrative, editorial pacing, human-centred ideas, reading experience, restraint, clarity, whitespace, warmth, and belonging. Do not copy their content, layouts, or visual identities.

## 7. Content priorities

Representative projects may include Human Agency Leadership Forum, She Rewires Singapore, She Rewires India, Women Beyond Wealth, and Emerging Markets Innovation Alliance. Select examples for meaning and brand evolution, not only scale.

People come first: founders, co-builders, partner leaders, community builders, and women innovators should be visible. The site should use real, rights-cleared photos and video wherever possible, with fewer posters and event promotional graphics.

Partners should be curated to approximately 10–15 approved logos rather than shown as an exhaustive wall.

Simple homepage proof may use: 80,000+ people, 150+ co-builders, 16+ cities, and One Global Movement.

## 8. Information architecture

The primary routes are:

- **About:** why we exist — story, evolution, philosophy
- **Community:** who is inside the system — co-builders, chapters, contributors, partners
- **Build:** what happens inside the system — model, projects, opportunities
- **Stories:** what emerges from the system — people, ideas, projects, conversations
- **Join Us:** how people enter the system — co-builder, chapter, partner, expertise, support paths

This route architecture should be preserved. Legacy event/media/contact routes may redirect into it.

## 9. CMS and launch boundaries

Founder-approved homepage content must support review. The eventual CMS must support preview, version history, and draft workflows. Editors may manage ordinary content, while core homepage and brand content remains subject to founder review.

August launch scope includes About, Community, Build, Stories, Join Us, Projects, Partners, CMS readiness, responsive behaviour, and SEO. Podcast, research, timeline, video library, and interactive map can follow.

Explicitly out of scope: membership systems, complex permissions, forums, and online courses.

## 10. Success criteria

Success is not simply showing what She Rewires has done. A first-time visitor should understand what She Rewires is and why it is worth co-building with within 30 seconds.

Priorities:

1. Accurately express the women-led co-building positioning and model.
2. Establish an international, long-term, thought-leadership brand.
3. Make participation paths clear to co-builders, partners, and supporters.
4. Keep the site capable of continuous content evolution.

## 12. Approval gates and launch decision

- **Before Phase 1:** founder alignment and homepage narrative are approved; a provisional legacy logo may remain; no final logo conversion is required to begin narrative implementation.
- **Before Phase 2:** the authoritative `.ai` source has been reviewed, export requirements are agreed, and the final logo variants are approved before visual refinement is shown to the founder.
- **Before Phase 3:** approved logo variants, approved imagery/partner assets, and usage rights are available for content integration.
- **Before motion implementation:** motion direction, reduced-motion behaviour, and logo/colour transitions are explicitly approved.

Unresolved launch decision: confirm whether August launch requires only the CMS-ready architecture or an operational CMS with preview, draft, version history, and editor workflow. Do not silently treat CMS readiness as operational CMS approval.
